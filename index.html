WEEKEND CHOICES

An interactive storytelling experience exploring the balance between productivity, rest, and emotional well-being during the weekend.

START EXPERIENCE

Story Background

For many students, weekends are both relaxing and stressful. Some people choose to study and complete their assignments, while others prefer to rest and recharge mentally after a busy week.

This interactive project explores two different choices that students often face during the weekend: continuing to work hard or taking time to relax.
